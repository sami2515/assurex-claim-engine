APPLICATION PIPELINE

How the AssureX Claim Engine Works

A rigorous 5-step processing pipeline designed strictly around Page 5 of the official architecture diagram.

1
Intake & Multi-Document Upload

Claimants submit product details and upload proof-of-purchase receipts, warranty cards, damage photos, and prior repair invoices through a 5-step guided intake wizard.

2
SHA-256 Hashing & OCR Extraction

Uploaded files are cryptographically fingerprinted with SHA-256 hashes to detect duplicate claims. Document OCR extracts invoice IDs, purchase dates, serial stamps, and retail vendors.

3
Dual-Branch AI Parallel Evaluation

Branch A (Python Tabular Random Forest) processes structured risk factors. Simultaneously, Branch B converts the claim into a standardized 640x420 Claim Summary Card evaluated by a Google Teachable Machine vision classifier.

4
Consensus Engine & Delta Δconf

The Dual-Model Comparator calculates top-class confidence divergence |Δconf| and assigns one of 5 formal statuses (Strong Match, Acceptable Match, Weak Match, Model Disagreement, Uncertain Result).

5
Configurable Policy & Fraud Gating

Business rules in category JSON schemas validate coverage duration, grace periods, authorized repair centers, excluded damage keywords, and chronological contradictions.

Master Adjudication & 8-Stage Tracker

Synthesizes the final decision into exactly 3 classes: Likely Valid, Likely Invalid, or Manual Review Required. Generates official ReportLab PDF certificates and updates the customer's 8-stage live tracker.

ENTERPRISE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Competition Rigor

Every feature is built strictly against the Aptech NextWave Software Requirements Specification (SRS Version 1.0).

Dual-Model Consensus

Combines tabular machine learning with visual card classification. Neither model holds absolute veto; decisions require bounded confidence and mutual class consensus.

Cryptographic Duplicate Detection

SHA-256 fingerprinting catches identical receipts submitted across different claims or users, blocking serial re-submissions and multi-channel warranty fraud.

Side-by-Side OCR Discrepancy Matching

Compares user-entered serial numbers and invoice dates directly with OCR extracted values, flagging discrepancies and chronological paradoxes for review.

Configurable Category Policies

JSON warranty policies for Consumer Electronics, Home Appliances, and Industrial Tools. Administrators can adjust grace periods and exclusion rules without redeploying code.

8-Stage Lifecycle State Machine

Tracks claims across 8 strict lifecycle stages: Draft → Submitted → Under Evaluation → Info Required → Manual Review → Approved → Rejected → Closed.

Official PDF Certificates

ReportLab engine generates official multi-page claim adjudication certificates with verification QR codes, complete rule breakdowns, and audit signatures.
