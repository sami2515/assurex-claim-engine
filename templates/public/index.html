Architecture Flowchart

The 5-Stage Adjudication Pipeline

Direct translation of the Page 5 system architecture diagram into an autonomous execution sequence.

01

Intake & Document Ingestion

Guided 5-step wizard captures equipment metadata, incident descriptions, purchase invoices, warranty certificates, and previous repair histories.

02

OCR & Cryptographic Hashing

Ingested files are fingerprinted with SHA-256 to stop duplicate submissions. OCR regex extracts invoice numbers, dates, and backplate serials for matching.

03

Dual AI Parallel Evaluation

Branch A evaluates 14 tabular risk metrics via Random Forest. Simultaneously, Branch B converts the dossier into a 640×420 visual card evaluated by Google Teachable Machine.

04

Consensus & Confidence Divergence

The Comparator assesses top-class agreement and calculates |Δconf|. Assigns 1 of 5 formal statuses (Strong Match, Acceptable Match, Weak Match, Model Disagreement, Uncertain Result).

05

Policy Gating & 8-Stage Tracker

Category rules (grace periods, damage exclusions, authorized centers) validate eligibility. Generates the final 3-class decision, ReportLab PDF certificate, and updates the live tracker.

Core Innovation

Dual-Branch Multimodal Consensus

Traditional warranty fraud models rely on single-algorithm classifiers that are prone to dataset shift. AssureX implements two independent branches that evaluate distinct representations of the same claim.

  • • Branch A: Tabular Random Forest processing numerical ratios and one-hot categorical vectors.
  • • Branch B: Google Teachable Machine vision classifier inspecting standardized 640×420 summary cards.
  • • Consensus Rule: Automated approval requires mutual agreement and |Δconf| ≤ 0.150. Disagreements automatically route to human reviewers.

Defense-in-Depth

Deterministic Policy Gating & Fraud Defense

AI predictions are cross-examined by deterministic business rules maintained in category-specific JSON schemas. No black-box model can override a policy exclusion or approved repair network mandate.

  • • Cryptographic Hashing: SHA-256 fingerprinting intercepts duplicate invoices and receipt re-submissions.
  • • Chronological Verification: Detects faults dated prior to retail purchase or past reporting deadlines.
  • • Configurable Policies: JSON rules for Consumer Electronics, Home Appliances, and Industrial Tools.
8-Stage Lifecycle State Machine
1. Draft → 2. Submitted → 3. Under Evaluation → 4. Info Required → 5. Manual Review → 6. Approved / 7. Rejected → 8. Closed
Strict state transitions prevent skipping verification stages or issuing automated approvals without complete evidence dossiers.
